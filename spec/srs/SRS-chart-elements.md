# SRS — Chart Elements

Requirements for the visual vocabulary of a chart: tasks, milestones, summary
rows, brackets, dependencies, and markers. Feature tag: `ELEM`.

Traces up to: `../data-model.md`, `../layout.md`, `../visual-vocabulary.md`.

## Tasks

|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-ELEM-001 | The system shall render each task as a horizontal bar positioned at its `start` date and spanning to its `end` date **inclusive** (a task with `start == end` occupies one whole day). | A Gantt bar's length is its duration; inclusive end matches user intent. | Test: `spec/fixtures/basic-chart` (t_d1 widthDays=8) | layout §4; `src/renderer/TaskBars.tsx`; `native/.../GanttLayout` |
| SRS-ELEM-002 | A task shall satisfy the invariant `end >= start` (duration ≥ 1 day inclusive); a task violating it is malformed. | Negative duration is meaningless. | Review (invariant) — **not yet enforced at load; see open items** | data-model §Task; SRS-EDIT-003 |
| SRS-ELEM-003 | When two tasks in the same row overlap in time, the system shall place them on separate stacking sub-rows so neither is occluded. | Overlapping work must remain readable. | Test: `basic-chart` (t_d1 subRow 0, t_d2 subRow 1) | layout §2; SRS-LAY-020 |
| SRS-ELEM-004 | The system shall fill a task bar with its `color` when set, else an implementation default accent. | Color encodes category; sensible default when unset. | Review | visual-vocabulary; data-model |

## Dependencies

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification | Trace |
|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-ELEM-040 | The system shall connect dependent tasks with an orthogonal (3-segment elbow) connector, with an arrowhead at the successor. | Standard Gantt dependency notation. | Test: `basic-chart` (dep1 fromXDay=12, toXDay=14) | layout §8; visual-vocabulary |
| SRS-ELEM-041 | The connector endpoints shall be selected by dependency `type`: finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish, start-to-finish. | The four link types attach to different bar ends. | Test: `tests/unit/dependencyRouting.test.ts` (all four types) | layout §8 |
| SRS-ELEM-042 | A connector's last segment shall travel into the successor from the side its link type names — rightwards into a `start` endpoint, leftwards into a `finish` endpoint — for every spacing of the two bars, including a zero gap and an overlap, so that the arrowhead always points at the successor. | A bar is painted through its end day (`width = endX - x + pxPerDay`), so when one task finishes on the day the next starts, the predecessor's right edge sits one day to the *right* of the successor's left edge. The elbow split at `midX = (fromX + toX) / 2`, which put the turn right of the successor's start; the final leg therefore ran right-to-left and `marker orient="auto"` rotated the arrowhead through 180°, pointing it back at the predecessor. Four of the six links in the shipped sample read backwards — `d="M 362.64 882 L 361.67 882 L 361.67 954 L 360.69 954"` on the running v2.21.0 build, a 1px leg pointing the wrong way. | Test: `tests/unit/dependencyRouting.test.ts`; `tests/e2e/dependency-arrows.spec.ts` | `layout/engine.ts` `dependencies`/`entryDir`; `renderer/Dependencies.tsx`; SR-IXC-01 (the chart must read as the schedule reads) |
| SRS-ELEM-043 | A connector shall leave its predecessor and enter its successor through a horizontal stub of at least 10px before it turns, and where the two endpoints leave no room for that stub it shall carry the crossing leg through the band between the two rows rather than along either bar. | With the turn at the midpoint the elbow collapsed to a hairline near-vertical jog with no clearance at either end: it left the predecessor's cap at the pixel it turned, and it ran straight down through whatever bar sat between the two rows. The owner called the result "messy" and could not tell which bar a connector belonged to. The stub is clearance, not obstacle avoidance — a connector still crosses a bar that happens to lie on its path, which the owner decided against routing around. | Test: `tests/unit/dependencyRouting.test.ts` | `layout/engine.ts` `DEP_STUB`/`DEP_LANE` |
| SRS-ELEM-044 | Every surface that draws a connector shall draw its arrowhead, the exported figure included. | `embed/GanttRenderer` drew the connector path and no head at all, so an exported or embedded chart showed undirected lines and a reader could not tell predecessor from successor. | Test: `tests/unit/dependencyRouting.test.ts` (entry direction the head is drawn from) | `embed/GanttRenderer.tsx` dependencies block; `renderer/Dependencies.tsx` `pp-arrow` |
| SRS-ELEM-045 `[web]` | A selected dependency shall offer delete and its four link types in the command bar. | `SelectionGroup` returned `null` for kind `dependency`, so clicking a connector selected it and left the bar empty. Delete and the link type existed only on the right-click menu; the owner found that menu and liked it, but the bar is where every other selection's one-press actions live, and a selection with no actions reads as a selection that did nothing. | Test: `tests/e2e/dependency-arrows.spec.ts` | `app/SelectionGroup.tsx` `DependencyFields`; DECISIONS.md D10 |

## Markers

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification | Trace |
|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-ELEM-050 | The system shall render a marker as a full-height vertical line at its `date` with its label, styled by `type` (today / deadline / note). | Markers call out dates spanning all rows. | Demo / Review | data-model; visual-vocabulary |
| SRS-ELEM-051 `[web]` | Every marker shall carry a pointer target along its rule and on its label, so that a click selects it, a right-click opens its menu and a drag moves it. | `Markers` drew a `<line>` and a `<text>` and nothing else — no `data-pp-kind`, no handler, no hit rect — so nothing about a marker could be hit. A deadline could be created and then never touched again: the context-menu rows for editing and deleting it existed and were unreachable, and the only thing in the product that could move one was the agent bridge. Owner report 2026-10-29: “Once I have created a deadline marker, I cannot edit the text, I cannot move it around.” SR-IXC-13 requires every supported gesture to have a visible affordance on the element itself. | Test: `tests/unit/markerFoot.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/marker-edit.spec.ts` | `renderer/Markers.tsx`; `useChartInteractions` §contextmenu; `ChartContextMenu` §marker |
| SRS-ELEM-052 `[web]` | A marker drag shall snap to the same whole-day grid as a bar drag, read out the date the release will commit while the pointer moves, and write exactly one undo frame when it is released. | The interaction hook’s own comment stated the gap — “Dependency and marker are selection-only (no drag)” — so a deadline’s date could only be changed by deleting the marker and placing another. SR-IXC-04 requires a live date pill on a point-date drag and SR-IXC-12 requires the snap to be active during the preview, not only at commit; the preview is held in the component and the document is written once on release, because `updateMarker` has no frame key and a write per `mousemove` would spend a 30-day move’s worth of the 100-frame history (SRS-EDIT-022). | Test: `tests/unit/markerFoot.test.ts` | `renderer/Markers.tsx` `markerDragDate`; `utils/snap` `snapDelta`; SRS-EDIT-002, SRS-EDIT-022 |
| SRS-ELEM-053 `[web]` | Marker labels shall be drawn as chips in one band at the foot of the chart, each centred on its own marker’s rule and inside the plot clip, and a double-click on a chip shall start an in-place rename of that marker. | The label was drawn at `y = 12` from the top of the rule, which is inside row 0: it sat on the first row’s bar, and with several markers in one month the labels overprinted each other. The owner’s own proposal is the fix — “all the labels for the deadline marker should appear at the bottom of the chart, and I should be able to select them, edit them, and move them around” — and it is the treatment approved in the design demo §07. Renaming had no route at all: the marker menu’s “Edit label” item only set the selection, so it opened nothing (SR-IXC-16, SR-IXC-20). | Test: `tests/unit/markerFoot.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/marker-edit.spec.ts` | `renderer/Markers.tsx` `footBandTop`; `app/InlineLabel`; `ChartContextMenu` §marker; `docs/mockup/web-selection-and-axis-mockup.html` §07 |
| SRS-ELEM-054 `[web]` | The foot band shall be drawn only while the document holds at least one marker, and shall name itself where it is drawn. | The approved demo states it: “the band only appears when something needs it.” A strip reserved at the foot of every chart whether or not anything is in it repeats the minimap’s mistake (SRS-ELEM-060), and the eyebrow is the one thing the minimap never had — anything parked at the foot of the chart has to say what it is. | Test: `tests/unit/markerFoot.test.ts` | `renderer/Markers.tsx`; SRS-ELEM-060 |

## Open items

- **SRS-ELEM-002 is not yet enforced:** neither `schema.ts` nor the JSON Schema
  rejects `end < start`, and the engine only clamps width (SRS-ELEM-007). Enforce
  the invariant when the JSON Schema becomes the validation source (Phase F2).
- The collapsed-group fixture is added in **F2** (conformance breadth);
  SRS-ELEM-021 cites it as a future test. The four link types no longer wait on
  a fixture — `tests/unit/dependencyRouting.test.ts` exercises each of them
  against the engine directly.
- SRS-ELEM-043 keeps a connector clear of its *own* bars only. A connector that
  crosses a third bar between the two rows still crosses it; routing around
  obstacles was declined by the owner on 2026-10-29.
- Percent-complete and label-placement currently verify by Demo/Review; promote
  to Test when a render-level fixture exists.

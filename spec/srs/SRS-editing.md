# SRS — Editing

Requirements for creating and editing a chart. These define *what* each edit
does, independent of surface; web implements them via direct manipulation, the
PowerPoint add-in reaches them in stages (see `SRS-powerpoint.md`). Feature tag:
`EDIT`.

Traces up to: `../interaction.md`, `../data-model.md`.

## Edit semantics

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification | Trace |
|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-EDIT-001 | All edits shall operate on the document and then re-run layout; no edit shall mutate device coordinates directly. | One source of truth; every surface stays consistent. | Review | interaction §core operations |
| SRS-EDIT-002 | Date edits shall snap to whole days (the abstract x unit). | Schedules are day-granular; sub-day positions are meaningless. | Review | interaction; layout §coordinates |
| SRS-EDIT-004 | The snap grid shall be one day on the day scale and one week on every coarser scale; it shall never be derived from the view scale. | How far a user is zoomed out is a property of the window, not of the calendar. Quantising to the view scale made a month view snap to 30 days, so on the shipped document every drag under a fortnight committed nothing and the bar sprang back. | Test (`snapDelta`) | utils/snap `snapDelta` |
| SRS-EDIT-005 | A drag preview shall show the snapped result, and the release shall commit exactly what the preview showed. | A bar that follows the pointer to the day and then jumps to a different date on release means the user never let go of the thing they got. | Test (e2e: no jump on release) | useChartInteractions `previewDelta` |
| SRS-EDIT-003 | Resize shall keep a task's `end` on or after its `start` (a resize cannot produce `end < start`). | Upholds the duration invariant during editing. | Demo (web resize) — promote to Test with an editing harness | data-model; SRS-ELEM-002 |

## Pointer affordance

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification | Trace |
|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-EDIT-030 `[web]` | Each interactive region shall carry the cursor that names the gesture it performs: `move` on a draggable body, `ew-resize` on a date edge, `col-resize` on the panel divider, `text` on an editable name, `grab`/`grabbing` only where the surface itself is panned. | The cursor is the only thing that tells a user what a region will do before they commit to a drag; a hand on a bar that moves a date promises panning and delivers a schedule change. | Test (e2e asserts the computed cursor) | TaskBars, Milestones, ChartArea |
| SRS-EDIT-031 `[web]` | A cursor shall change to its active form while the gesture is in progress, and return when it ends. | Without it there is no feedback that a drag was picked up, only that something moved. | Test (e2e) | useChartInteractions |
| SRS-EDIT-032 `[web]` | No interactive region shall carry the default arrow cursor where a gesture exists, and no non-interactive region shall carry a gesture cursor. | A cursor that lies in either direction costs a user a wasted drag and the undo that follows. | Test (e2e sweeps the surface) | renderer/* |
| SRS-EDIT-033 `[web]` | Every pointer gesture shall convert client coordinates to chart coordinates through one shared geometry source read at the moment of the event — row-gutter width, header height, vertical scroll, plot width and the current layout — so that the press, the move, the hit test and the drawn overlay cannot disagree; a marquee sweep shall select exactly the elements the drawn rectangle covers. | Four hand-rolled copies of the same subtraction drifted apart: the lasso's moving edge landed 80px from the pointer, because the move handler was pinned by an empty dependency array to the first render's 120px gutter against the 200px the chart draws; no gesture read `scrollY`, so a scrolled sweep was drawn and tested hundreds of pixels above the pointer; and the hit test ran against a layout captured before the initial fit, so after a zoom a sweep took a bar it did not cover and missed one it did. | Test: `tests/e2e/marquee-geometry.spec.ts`, `tests/unit/geometry.test.ts` | `layout/geometry.ts` `clientToChart`/`chartToClient`; `useChartInteractions`; `ChartArea` `geomRef` |

## Keyboard

|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-EDIT-040 `[web]` | Every keyboard binding shall be declared once in a single keymap table as a full chord stating each of Ctrl/Cmd, Alt and Shift as required or forbidden, and a key press shall run only the binding whose chord it matches exactly; an unmatched chord shall run nothing and suppress nothing. | Bindings matched by an if-chain of bare letters let an earlier branch claim a chord meant for a later one and return before it. ## Selection & history

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification | Trace |
|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-EDIT-020 | The system shall support selecting one element and extending the selection to multiple. | Bulk operations need multi-select. | Demo | interaction §core operations |
| SRS-EDIT-021 | Edits shall be undoable/redoable through the host's history. | Safe experimentation. | Demo | interaction §undo |
| SRS-EDIT-022 | One continuous input gesture — a name typed into a field, a percent slider dragged, a date typed, a nudge key tapped in one direction — and one multi-item command shall each produce exactly one undo frame, however many characters, steps or items it covers. | Undo is the app's only recovery path and it was spent on characters: `mutate` pushed a frame on every call and the store had no notion of a gesture, so a 12-character rename cost 12 frames and a 120-character note evicted the whole 100-frame history. The agent path already had this guarantee (SRS-ABR-013); the user's did not. | Test: `tests/e2e/undo-granularity.spec.ts`, `tests/unit/undoFrames.test.ts` | documentStore `mutate` key / `transact` / `endFrame`; DetailsDialog; Header `TitleChip`; keymap `nudge`; documentActions `deleteSelection` |
| SRS-EDIT-023 `[web]` | Every surface that draws selection state shall subscribe to the selected items themselves, not to a predicate over them, so that selecting and deselecting repaint the chart within the same frame as the state change. | The five chart renderers each read `useSelectionStore((s) => s.isSelected)`. That selector returns a function created once by `create()`, so its reference never changes, Zustand's equality check always passes and the component never re-renders. The chrome was correct and simply never painted: a click selected a bar and drew nothing, Escape deselected it and left the outline on screen, and both only resolved when an unrelated redraw happened to occur. The status bar and the command bar subscribe to `items` and did update, which is why the owner saw the footer react while the chart did not. | Test: `tests/unit/selectionRepaint.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/selection-paint.spec.ts` | `renderer/TaskBars`, `RowGutter`, `Milestones`, `Brackets`, `Dependencies`; `stores/selectionStore` `useIsSelected` |
| SRS-EDIT-024 `[web]` | A selected row shall be banded across the full width of the chart, gutter and plot together, with its accent edge at the left margin. | Only the gutter cell was tinted, so at any useful zoom the band ended at the row labels and the eye had to track a bar across an untinted plot to know which row it belonged to. | Test: `tests/e2e/selection-paint.spec.ts` | `renderer/RowGutter`; `renderer/RowBands`; `ChartArea` |
| SRS-EDIT-025 `[web]` | A click on empty chart space, on empty gutter space, or the Escape key shall clear the selection, and no armed insert tool shall prevent it. | While an insert tool was armed every background click created another item instead of deselecting, so the only route back to an empty selection was a key the user had not been told about. | Test: `tests/e2e/selection-paint.spec.ts` | `useChartInteractions` background branch; `keymap` Escape |
| SRS-EDIT-026 `[web]` | An insert tool shall revert to the select tool once it has inserted one item. | `setTool` defaulted to sticky and every caller took the default, so pressing `T` armed task insertion permanently: three clicks meant to deselect produced three tasks, and the identical branch produced two milestones. | SRS-EDIT-027 `[web]` | While an insert tool is armed the command bar shall name it, and the naming shall clear when the tool reverts. | The only cue that the chart was in an insert mode was a crosshair cursor, and the tool row is replaced by the selection group as soon as the inserted item is selected, so the mode was invisible at exactly the moment it was doing damage. | Test: `tests/e2e/insert-tool.spec.ts` | `CommandBar` armed chip; `stores/toolStore` |
| SRS-EDIT-028 `[web]` | An insert shall land on the selected row; where a section row is selected it shall land on a new row created directly beneath it, and the inserted item shall be scrolled into view and left selected. | Both the insert buttons and the insert keys passed `doc.rows[0]`, which in every shipped template is a section header and is usually off-screen, so an insert appeared to do nothing or to attach work to a heading. | Test: `tests/unit/insertTarget.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/insert-tool.spec.ts` | `CommandBar` insert group; `keymap` insert entries; `useChartInteractions` |
| SRS-EDIT-029 `[web]` | The command bar shall not restate the name of the selected item. | The bar repeated a name the chart was failing to show, which made it the only indication of what was selected. With the chart repainting it is duplication, and it cost the width that the bar's actual controls needed. | Test: `tests/e2e/selection-paint.spec.ts` | `SelectionGroup` |

## Moving, resizing and progress

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification | Trace |
|----|-------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| SRS-EDIT-070 `[web]` | The two ends of a selected task bar shall carry its resize grips, and the bar's dependency anchors shall be drawn clear of those ends on a short lead, sharing no pixel with a grip. | The anchors were centred on the bar's end caps — `cx={x}` and `cx={x + width}` at `r=5` — which is the only place a resize grip can go, and each 5px grip sat entirely under one of them. So resizing worked only while the bar was *not* selected, and selecting a bar in order to resize it took the grips away: "we should first select, but then when we select, we have the anchor to do the dependency… we are missing that option to resize." | SRS-EDIT-071 `[web]` | A press shall be resolved to the most specific chart element under the pointer, not to whichever element happens to be painted last. | Moving the anchors was only half of it. `Dependencies` renders after `TaskBars` and lays a transparent 10px-wide `stroke` along each connector purely as a hit target, so wherever a connector passed over a bar end that invisible band took the press: the resize grip was unreachable and the gesture selected the dependency instead.
